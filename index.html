The Simple Truth: Mealworms Practically Raise Themselves

Here's what nobody tells you about mealworm farming: It's not farming. It's harvesting from a self-replenishing system.

Once you set up your initial colony (30 minutes), the mealworms do the work. They eat, they grow, they pupate, they reproduce.

Your job?

  • Add food once a week (oatmeal or wheat bran—$4/month)
  • Check moisture (slice of potato/carrot every few days)
  • Harvest mature worms (5 minutes)

That's it.

A single bin produces 1–2 cups/week within 60 days. At $8–$12/cup, that's $32–$96/month from one bin.
